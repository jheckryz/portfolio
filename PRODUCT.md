# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: prospective freelance clients (people or small businesses who might pay Jheckryz to build something). They arrive with a need, judge credibility quickly from the work shown, and decide whether to get in touch.

Open decision: the user later supplied a list of required sections (certifications, course completions, IoT, capstone and third-year projects, achievements) that reads like an academic requirement. Whether school reviewers are also a primary audience has not been confirmed.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Jheckryz Maison Osio. It introduces who they are, shows what they have built and what they can build with, and gets a prospective client to make contact. Success means a client understands the offer and reaches out by email or social link.

## Positioning

Open decision: the specific freelance offer (what is sold, to whom) has not been stated and must not be invented. Confirmed so far: a BS Information Technology student at St. Dominic College of Asia, interested in problem-solving across software development, web design, cybersecurity and data management.

## Operating Context

Visitors reach the site from a shared link, most likely on a phone or laptop. Contact happens outside the site, through email, GitHub or LinkedIn.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step.
- One long page in `index.html`: Hero, About, Works (DominiMap, then More Projects), Tech Stack, Certifications & Achievements, Contact. The nav jumps between sections.
- There is no form backend. Contact is links only, and the site must never claim a message was sent.
- The existing light/dark theme toggle stays.
